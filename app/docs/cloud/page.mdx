Caveman Cloud is the managed plane for a fleet: one view across every agent and developer, spend attributed to the project, person or branch that produced it, and optimizations that run behind eval gates. It is in private development, and the waitlist is open at [caveman.so](https://caveman.so), where it is also called the Caveman Platform.

Want compression on your own machine? That needs no account and no waitlist; start at the [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart).

## What it is for

A local tool sees your bytes, not your invoice. It can say a payload got smaller; what that was worth depends on the model, the region, the cache state at the moment of the call and your contract, which is the second rule on [Numbers and limits](/docs/counting#the-four-rules). It also saw one arm of the comparison, so it cannot say whether the smaller payload produced the same answer. Cloud holds the price table, runs both arms, and compares against traffic that actually happened.

| What it adds | |
|---|---|
| Fleet visibility | One view across every agent, developer and workflow, instead of one database per laptop. |
| Spend attribution | Provider-reported usage priced against the public model catalog. A subtotal, not an invoice, and an unknown model stays visibly unpriced. |
| Eval gates | An optimization reaches traffic only after a grader passed on that traffic. |

## Where `verified` comes from

A saving is promoted one rung at a time: `inferred`, then replayed, then `verified`. A number arriving from a laptop stays `inferred`, because crossing a network promotes nothing. `verified` is reserved for a saving confirmed against a real bill by the system that ran both arms, and it starts at zero and moves only on provider-causal evidence. [Numbers and limits](/docs/counting#verified) defines the word for the whole site.
